rules summary for hosts

What your players are doing

Each team runs a chip company for 6 rounds. Up to 4 people can share control of one company — on the join screen, they can start a new one or join an existing one that has an open seat. Consider reading this out loud (or sharing your screen) before you hit Start — first-timers won't know any of this yet.

1
Invest

Teams spend cash on Tech (helps their score this round) or Capacity (more chips to sell next round). Optional, and permanent once locked in — they can undo a purchase before then.

2
Price & quantity, live

Everyone sees everyone else's numbers update in real time on the ticker board — that's the bluffing/negotiation part. Each team also gets a private Team channel plus an All-Teams channel where only each team's leader can post (everyone can still read it) — you can see and post in the All-Teams one below. They can change their numbers until they lock in.

3
Apple buys

When the timer hits zero (or everyone locks in early), Apple buys from the best-scoring sellers first until its demand for the round runs out. Leftover chips carry over. Players can see the total demand pool themselves — no need to read it off this screen for them.

4
After Round 6

Highest total Company Value (cash + leftover chips + tech + capacity) wins. You control pacing with Pause / Resume / Force skip below.

Every round also rolls one random Market Shock that temporarily changes the rules — e.g. a supplier outage means fewer chips get made, or Apple suddenly cares more about Tech. It's what stops the same build order from winning every match. Players see one plain sentence explaining it in real-world terms; you see the exact numbers behind it too.
