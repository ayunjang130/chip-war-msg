how to play · 30 seconds

You run a chip company.

Over 6 rounds, you're competing with other companies to win Apple's purchase orders. Whoever builds the most valuable company by the end wins.

1
Invest

Spend cash to raise your Tech level (helps your score immediately) or your Capacity level (more chips to sell, starting next round). Both are optional every round.

2
Set your price & quantity

Pick how much to charge and how many chips to offer. You can see everyone else's live numbers on the ticker board — and they can see yours. A small badge under the price box shows your predicted rank in real time (🟢 safe, 🟡 partial sale, 🔴 risk) so you're never guessing. Change your mind as many times as you want.

3
Lock In

Once you're happy with your numbers, lock them in. When everyone's locked (or time runs out), Apple buys from the best-scoring sellers first — a mix of low price, high tech, and high capacity — until it runs out of demand for that round.

6
After Round 6

Whoever has the highest total Company Value (cash + leftover chips + tech + capacity, all counted as money) wins the match.

⚡ Each round also gets one random Market Shock shown at the top of the screen (e.g. "Capacity efficiency -40%" or "Apple values Tech 2x this round"). It changes the rules just for that round, so the exact same strategy won't win every match.
Tip: cheap pricing alone won't win — a rival with better Tech or Capacity can out-score you even at a higher price. There's no single best strategy.
