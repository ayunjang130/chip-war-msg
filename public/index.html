how to play · 30 seconds

You run a chip company.

Over 6 rounds, you compete against other companies to win Apple's purchase orders. The company with the highest value at the end wins.

1
Invest (optional)

Spend cash to raise your Tech level, which improves your score this round, or your Capacity level, which produces more chips starting next round. You can undo any purchase from this round until you lock in.

2
Set your price & quantity

Choose how much to charge and how many chips to offer. Everyone's numbers update live, so you can watch your rivals and react. A colored badge shows your predicted rank in real time.

3
Lock In

When you're ready, lock in your numbers — they become final for the round. Once everyone has locked in (or the timer runs out), Apple buys from the highest-scoring sellers first until it runs out of demand.

4
After Round 6

The company with the highest total value — cash, unsold chips, Tech, and Capacity combined — wins the match.

⚡ Watch for a random Market Shock each round — it changes the rules just for that round, so no single strategy wins every match. Prices are capped at 3x the last market price, so there's room for a premium play, just no absurd numbers.
