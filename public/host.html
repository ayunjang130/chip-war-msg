rules summary for hosts

What your players are doing

Each team runs a chip company for 6 rounds. Consider reading this out loud (or sharing your screen) before you hit Start — first-timers won't know any of this yet.

1
Invest

Teams spend cash on Tech (helps their score this round) or Capacity (more chips to sell next round). Optional, and permanent once bought.

2
Price & quantity, live

Everyone sees everyone else's numbers update in real time on the ticker board — that's the bluffing/negotiation part. They can change their numbers until they lock in.

3
Apple buys

When the timer hits zero (or everyone locks in early), Apple buys from the best-scoring sellers first — a mix of low price, high tech, high capacity — until its demand for the round runs out. Leftover chips carry over.

6
After Round 6

Highest total Company Value (cash + leftover chips + tech + capacity) wins. You control pacing with Pause / Resume / Force skip below.
